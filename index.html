Design principles

Geography first

Every resource targets spatial reasoning, place-based analysis, and geographical inquiry. Not generic education prompts with geography labels.

Tool-agnostic

Works with any AI tool. No accounts, no subscriptions, no specific platform. Paste a prompt and go.

Honest about limits

AI produces geography slop: text that looks like the discipline but doesn't do what the discipline does. This toolkit helps you see it and address it.

About

Patrick Ell is a K–12 AI Integration Lead based in Sydney. He works across a school community to support teachers in using AI as a cognitive co-worker: a tool that extends professional capability without replacing professional judgement.

This toolkit was built for the GTA NSW/ACT Annual Conference 2026. The talk it accompanies is Keeping Geography at the Centre: Strategic AI Integration for Meaningful Learning.
